Unit 2 — Securing Spaces

~21 class periods · Analyze Risk · Mitigate Risk · Detect Attacks
UNIT FOUNDATION

Physical security is the first layer of defense-in-depth

Adversaries who gain physical access to a device can often bypass many technical controls protecting that device and its data. For every Unit 2 scenario, reason through:

ASSETWhat valuable system, data, device, service, or property is at stake?
VULNERABILITYWhat physical weakness exposes it?
THREATWho or what could exploit the weakness?
ATTACKWhat specific mechanism could be used?
RISKWhat are the likelihood and severity?
MITIGATIONWhich control directly reduces the risk?
DETECTIONWhere will evidence of the attack appear?
RESILIENCEWhat layer remains if one control is bypassed?
